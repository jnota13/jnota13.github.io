About you

Age feeds the risk check. Weight and sex go in the summary you can show a doctor. The taper itself is built from what you actually drink, not from body weight.

What you drink now

If you drink more than one type, enter the total in U.S. standard drinks/day. One U.S. standard drink contains about 0.6 fl oz (14 g) of pure alcohol: 12 fl oz beer at 5% ABV, 5 fl oz wine at 12% ABV, or 1.5 fl oz liquor at 40% ABV (80 proof). At 20% ABV (40 proof), about 3 fl oz contains one standard drink. Check the label: actual strength and serving sizes vary.

Safety

Call 911 right away for
  • A seizure, or another seizure
  • Hallucinations, confusion, or not knowing where you are
  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, or a racing or irregular heartbeat
  • Heavy drowsiness, trouble waking, or fainting
  • Fever of 100.4°F or higher with shaking or sweating

Get help the same day for

  • Shakes that keep getting worse
  • Repeated vomiting, or a headache with confusion
  • Upper belly pain that gets worse
  • Yellow skin or eyes, or dark urine
SAMHSA helpline, free, 24/7: 1-800-662-4357

Timing to know

  • Withdrawal symptoms can start within hours of a drop in drinking.
  • Seizures show up most often between 8 and 48 hours after the last drink.
  • Delirium tremens usually starts around 48 hours and can run up to five days. It can be fatal without treatment.

While you taper

  • Ask a healthcare professional whether you need thiamine; do not rely on supplements to prevent severe withdrawal.
  • Eat regular meals and drink plenty of water.
  • Don't drive if you have symptoms.
  • Keep someone in the loop, especially in the first two weeks.
  • A clinician can assess withdrawal risk and discuss appropriate treatment options.
